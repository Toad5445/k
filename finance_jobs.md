**How each posting was checked:**
- I read the full description from the employer's own job feed (Greenhouse, Lever or Workday) or from the full LinkedIn or Built In listing, not a search-result snippet.
- The experience line below is quoted word for word.
- I re-checked every posting on Oct 14, 2026 and found it still live: no "No longer accepting applications" banner on LinkedIn, and still listed on the employer's site or Built In.
- I checked each one for an experience floor of 0, the right graduation cycle, no internship/temp/contract/commission arrangement, no accounting duties, no Python/SQL/Master's/CFA/CPA requirement, and location, and against your already-seen list.

**Sources searched:** LinkedIn (about 1,700 entry-level, full-time postings across 26 finance title searches in NYC, Westchester, Stamford, Danbury and US-remote), Built In (remote and NYC entry-level finance), and the Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday job boards of a few hundred finance and fintech employers.

**Remote roles are scarce.** Nearly every remote entry-level finance listing I opened was hybrid, excluded New York, required 1+ years, or was an AI-training gig. Only one remote role passed, and it carries a caveat.
